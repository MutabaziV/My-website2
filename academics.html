Evaluation

Grading System: We follow the National TVET qualification framework. Grades are based on both practical assessments (60%) and written exams (40%).

Attendance: A minimum of 85% attendance is required to sit for final exams.

Key Dates

Term 1: Oct - Dec
Term 2: Jan - April
Term 3: April - July

Note: External National Exams usually take place during the third term.
