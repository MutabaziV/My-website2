Entry Requirements

To join our programs, applicants must have successfully completed their secondary school education. For advenced level applicants should provide a Senior Three (S3) certificate .

How to Apply

Prospective students can apply directly at our campus in Musanze or online. Please ensure you have a copy of your National ID, academic transcripts, and two recent passport photos.

Fees & Support

MIPC offers flexible tuition payment plans to support our students. Contact the bursar's office for a detailed fee structure and scholarship opportunities.
