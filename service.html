Training

Short Courses: We offer intensive certificate programs in ICT, Masonry, and Culinary Arts designed for immediate employment.

Industrial Attachment: Students are placed in top Musanze-based companies to gain real-world professional experience.

Workshops: Regular guest lectures from industry experts to keep students updated on modern technology.

Club & Sport

Sports: Football and Basketball teams competing at the provincial level. Our field offers great views of the volcanoes!

Clubs: Join the Coding Club, Debate Society, or our Environmental Conservation group.

Resource Center & Library

E-Library: 24/7 access to digital textbooks and research papers. Our physical library in Musanze provides a quiet study space with over 5,000 reference books.
